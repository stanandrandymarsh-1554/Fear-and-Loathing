      FEAR&LOATHING

      a savage journey to the heart of the deal

      There is no honest way to explain the itinerary, because the man who wrote it down was not in any condition to be writing things down.

      You have a list of errands and a head full of bad chemistry. Sober, you are too afraid to speak to anyone. Loaded, you cannot read your own words. Find the middle. There is no middle.

      In the case: grass, mescaline, blotter acid, cocaine, a galaxy of uppers and downers, tequila, raw ether, amyls. Adrenochrome is not in the case. You have to find somebody willing to sell it.

      ETHER & DOWNERS no fear · a slower clock take your legs, not your eyes. you keep walking where you were already walking
      MESCALINE & ACID perception · you see what is there and a great deal that is not. the whole light show lives here
      COKE · UPPERS · AMYLS nerve, and coke alone makes you articulate you twitch, you cannot hold an aim, and it all wears off fast
      GRASS & TEQUILA the edge off, cheaply blur, doubling, a late hand
      the floor is a floor: play the slots at five dollars a pull, buy a drink at the revolving bar, and take the elevator only when you are finished — there is no coming back down. After the floor there is the room, after the room there is the road, and at the end of the road, if you answer the telephone, four hundred district attorneys.
